# Team Top 5 Adjusted Bonus Live Proj. Final Pool %

Ranking by Top 5 (lowest 5 scores per team). Adjusted = Top 5 + Bonuses. Bonuses (projected from current standings): −5 per tournament winner, −1 per top-10 finish—these don't stack, so a winner gets −5, not −6 (bonuses count all 10 players). Missed-cut players use the worst made-cut score for top-5 ranking. Live = sum of scores for players who made the cut only. Proj. Final = median projected final adjusted score from 3,000 Monte Carlo sims, with 25th–75th percentile range in parentheses. Pool % = each team's probability of winning the pool from the same simulations. Player win probabilities are sourced live from Polymarket (falling back to draft-pick priors if unavailable); remaining scoring variance scales with holes left.
